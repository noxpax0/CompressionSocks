# Privacy and retention

Mercury stores potentially sensitive health information locally. The operator remains responsible for lawful collection, access control, retention, disclosure, and deletion under applicable policy and law.

Data is encrypted for the signed-in Windows user. Completed orders remain in local history under the organisation’s retention policy. Back up the device with an approved encrypted system; DPAPI-protected data is not a portable export format.

The WhatsApp supplier summary omits patient name, telephone number, notes, and measurements. Review the preview and recipient before sending. Printing and clipboard actions may expose data outside Mercury. Technical logs do not intentionally record form fields or full WebView messages.
